Dividend Strategy

Renda Portfolio

This portfolio has a focus on the dividend-pioneering philosophy of Luiz Barsi, targeting intrinsically valuable, cash-generating companies in the Brazilian stock market. The strategy programmatically selects fundamentally sound enterprises trading at attractive margins of safety that yield sustainable, high payouts.
The strategy has an average rebalancing period of 6 to 12 months with a universe of stocks focused on:

B3 Dividends Value MoS

Use cases

  • Programmatic passive income generation
  • Fundamental equity screening
  • Defensive capital preservation

Trade offs

Strengths

  • + Consistent cash flow via optimized dividend yield
  • + High margin of safety from strictly buying underpriced assets
  • + Tailored to capitalize on the unique tax-exempt dividend structure in Brazil

Weaknesses

  • - High concentration in cyclical sectors (Utilities, Financials, Commodities)
  • - Sensitive to local regulatory, fiscal, and macroeconomic policy shifts
  • - Lower capital appreciation potential during rapid growth market cycles
